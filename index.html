02 / About

Design that feels clear, useful and finished.

I’m Minh Nguyen, an Austin-based graphic designer with more than a decade of experience in advertising, print, branding, web design, marketing, photography, and illustration. Currently working in the advertising and publishing industry, I create effective visual solutions for a wide range of businesses while collaborating with sales and creative teams to bring ideas from concept to final production. My background spans commercial real estate marketing, in-house design, photography, digital campaigns, and print production. I earned my BFA in Communication Design from Texas State University and approach every project with the same goal: create work that looks great, communicates clearly, and serves a purpose.
